CHARACTER IMPACT / PERSONAL NOTES

The people I borrowed something from.

This is not a power ranking. It is the part of the archive about what stayed with me after the episode or chapter ended: loyalty, discipline, resilience, emotional control, hope, second chances and the tiny habits that somehow follow you back into real life.

01 / CHARACTER IMPACT
North star / favorite in all fiction

Luffy

The GOAT for me. What makes Luffy special is not just the gears, fights or ridiculous move names. It is the way he stays loyal to his morals, protects his people at any cost, keeps the same dream even when the world laughs, and keeps moving after moments that should have broken him. He also helped me loosen the grip of overthinking and enjoy the part of life that is actually happening now.

People’s dreams never end.
Dream loudlyProtect your peopleNever give upGo with the flow
The Wano fits and Gear 2 → Gear 5 hype are absolutely part of the case too.
02 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Emotional core / crush in all fiction

Robin

Yes, she is my fictional crush. But the story is why she stayed. Robin survives Ohara, years of being hunted and the belief that people will eventually abandon her. Then the Straw Hats show her the opposite: she is worth crossing the world for. Enies Lobby turns that entire life of running into one request to keep living, and that is the moment she completely stole my heart.

“I want to live.”
BelongingSurvivalHopeFound family
For me, Robin is proof that finding the right people can change the meaning of everything that came before.
03 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Will without noise

Zoro

Zoro does not need a giant speech every episode. His sheer will, refusal to quit and unwavering loyalty to his captain do the talking. The lesson I take from him is simple: keep aiming higher, keep going, and one day you will look back and barely recognize how far you have climbed.

DisciplineLoyaltyAim higher
04 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Endure

Guts

Emotionally and psychologically, one of the strongest journeys in fiction for me. The amount of trauma and cruelty thrown at Guts is almost absurd, but the point is that suffering never gets the final word. He keeps prevailing. Also, the armor, the presence and that gigantic sword are just ridiculously cool.

ResilienceKeep movingDefy the dark
05 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Control before reaction

Ayanokoji

I never expected a manipulative high-school character to land this high for me. What I actually admire is his emotional control, awareness, selective self-revelation and ability to handle complex situations without needing attention. I do not admire treating people like tools, but the composure and decision-making are areas I want to sharpen in myself.

AwarenessComposureRead the roomKnow what to reveal
06 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Try again, even when nobody remembers

Subaru Natsuki

I completely misjudged him at first. What looked like weakness became the exact reason I respected him: Subaru can be terrified, humiliated, broken and still walk back into a nightmare because the people around him matter more than his pride. Return by Death is not a cool reset button to me anymore. It is loneliness with a checkpoint.

Fine. One more try.
Try againEmotional courageCarry the unseenStay human
Biggest opinion reversal in the archive. The charges have officially been withdrawn. 😂
07 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Symbol of hope

All Might

All Might works for me because of what he represents. People see him and believe help has arrived. That final stand and iconic raised fist hit harder than a long personality essay ever could. Sometimes what you symbolize to other people matters just as much as what you say.

HopePresenceReassurance
08 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Face reality

Rudeus

A deeply flawed guy, but the second-chance premise left me with a very real lesson: real life does not hand us a reincarnation button. Eventually we have to stop running from our problems, accept who we are, face reality head-on and try to live without regrets.

Face itNo reset buttonLive deliberately
The three wives are not the lesson. That part is just Rudeus winning the lottery. 😭
09 / CHARACTER IMPACT
Respect the craft

Sanji

Sanji is a hardworking cook who gives his best, carries two backstories and, yes, is still a catastrophic simp. The most practical thing he left with me is respect for food: do not waste what is on the table, because there is real work and value behind every meal.

Hard workRespect foodGive your best

THE LUFFY STANDARD

The benchmark.

Luffy is the character I end up comparing everyone else against, not because he is perfect, but because his values barely move when life gets ugly. He dreams loudly, protects his people without hesitation, follows his heart, gets back up after being shattered, and somehow makes freedom feel practical instead of abstract.

Dream without apologyIf the dream matters, say it out loud even when people laugh.
Protect your peopleLoyalty means showing up when it is costly, not only when it is convenient.
Move before overthinkingNot every uncertainty deserves a hundred imaginary disasters before the first step.
Turn pain into strengthAfter breaking, rebuild with a reason to become stronger rather than staying inside the loss.
THE ROBIN CORNER / DIPLOMATIC IMMUNITY

The one who stole the archive.

Yes, she is the all-fiction crush. But the reason Robin stayed with me is much bigger than that. Her story is about surviving a world that taught her she should not exist, then slowly discovering people who refuse to let her disappear. Intelligence, calmness, dark humor, resilience and found family all meet in one character.

The moment that sealed it: Enies Lobby. After spending years believing she was a burden, she finally allowed herself to say that she wanted to live. That scene is the point where Robin stopped being just a character I liked and became one I genuinely cared about.
MOMENTS THAT CHANGED SOMETHING

Scenes I carried back into real life.

Not necessarily the biggest fights or the prettiest animation. These are the beats that left behind an idea, a habit, or a way of looking at things after the episode ended.

01 / LUFFY

After Ace

Watching Luffy reach the point where even his dream felt meaningless, then remember the people still counting on him, made his recovery hit harder than any victory.

What stayed: being broken is not the same as being finished.
02 / ROBIN

“I want to live.”

Her fear was not only death. It was becoming such a burden that the people she loved would eventually hate her. The Straw Hats answered that fear by choosing her anyway.

What stayed: sometimes hope begins with allowing yourself to ask for it.
03 / ALL MIGHT

The final standing pose

What I remember most is not the mechanics of the battle. It is what he represented to everyone watching him: the idea that one person can become a symbol of reassurance.

What stayed: hope can matter even before it solves anything.
04 / GUTS

Keep moving anyway

His story keeps stacking trauma on top of trauma, yet the part that matters to me is that he keeps prevailing rather than letting suffering become his final identity.

What stayed: resilience does not have to look graceful.
05 / AYANOKOJI

Control before reaction

The trait I keep returning to is his ability to stay composed, reveal only what he chooses, and understand a situation before advertising what he feels.

What stayed: emotional control creates room to think.
06 / ZORO

Aim higher

Zoro is less about speeches for me and more about sheer will, loyalty and continually raising the ceiling of what he expects from himself.

What stayed: progress can look impossible only because you have not reached the future version of yourself yet.

THE JOURNEY

How the rabbit hole happened.

Not a clean genre progression. More like one door opening into eight completely different rooms.

PHASE 00 · THE DOOR CRACKS OPEN
2023
Berserk: The Golden Age

One of my first proper steps into anime, right after my A/Ls. I did not become an anime fan overnight, but Guts planted one idea that stayed with me: life can be brutally unfair and you can still keep moving.

You do not need an easy life to keep moving forward.
ResilienceGutsThe seed
PHASE 01 · POINT OF NO RETURN
July 2024
One Piece

I finished the available story in roughly three months and immediately regretted speedrunning it. Nothing felt the same afterward. Luffy turned dreaming loudly from something I found ridiculous into something I deeply respect.

Dream loudly. Protect your people. Keep moving.
10/103× rewatchesLife-changing
PHASE 02 · CHASING THE HIGH
Nov 2024 → early 2025
AOT → JJK → Demon Slayer

This run helped me discover my personal filter. Beautiful animation can absolutely get my attention, but it cannot make me care by itself. I need the story and characters to leave something behind.

Pretty animation can get my attention. Emotion is what makes me stay.
PHASE 03 · RETURNING TO THE GRAND LINE
Apr → Dec 2025
One Piece rewatch #2

This time I watched with my sister. Suddenly I was not just revisiting scenes, I was waiting for somebody else to reach the emotional landmines while pretending to act normal. The story became part of family life too.

Family memoryRewatch changed the story
PHASE 04 · THE GRIND ERA
Jan → Mar 2026
Black Clover

Asta is basically a walking refusal to accept the limit he was given. Loud? Extremely. But underneath it is something I respect: work until the impossible becomes less impossible.

Outwork the limitWatched with my sister
PHASE 05 · WHY IS THIS SCHOOL ANIME SO GOOD?
2026
Classroom of the Elite

Ayanokoji became one of my favorites because of awareness, composure, observation and control over what he reveals. I want the discipline, not the dehumanization.

Take the discipline. Leave the dehumanization.

PHASE 06 · TRAUMA, RESET, REPEAT
June 2026 → present
Re:Zero

I started by calling Subaru a dumb simp and ended up respecting him for exactly the emotions I first mistook for weakness. Return by Death stopped looking like a power and started looking like a curse nobody else can remember.

Fine. One more try.

My first proper romance anime dismantled another assumption. The relationship felt sincere and emotionally mature enough that I went straight into the manga afterward.

If the story is good, the genre does not matter.

MY PERSONAL TASTE FORMULA

What earns a place.

✅ Usually works on me
✦Strong emotion that feels earned.
✦Characters with conviction.
✦Worldbuilding and lore that reward attention.
✦Unpredictable stories and psychological tension.
✦Long-term growth, failure, suffering and adaptation.
❌ Fastest ways to lose me
×Generic, template-like stories.
×Plots where I do not care about anybody.
×Predictability disguised by pretty production.
×Development that was never earned.
×A huge build-up followed by a fumbled payoff.
Animation can impress me.
Characters and emotion are what make me remember.
